## Overview

Xhubantu is an event-based social networking platform that brings event discovery, ticketing, and content media sharing into one hub. It includes chatting and feed sharing to enable connection beyond events.

## Vision

The philosophy behind it: the next wave of social media will be event apps that enable people to connect more in physical and genuine meetups. Not feeds for feeds' sake, but gatherings that create real proximity.

## Key Features

- **Event discovery**: Find and explore events around you.
- **Ticketing**: Discover and manage event tickets in one place.
- **Content and media sharing**: Share moments and media tied to real-world gatherings.
- **Chatting and feeds**: Stay connected beyond the event itself.

## Technologies Used

Powered by a modern stack spanning frontend, backend and mobile.
