## What is Civilization

Civilization refers to a complex human society characterized by the development of urban centers, advanced social structures, specialized labor, centralized governance, and cultural achievements such as writing, art, and technology.

It is hard and totally impossible for one human to run the world without everyone else because we just can't do everything; manufacture, maintain, repair and protect. So, it is inevitable for humans to collaborate and work together to make survival (the primary goal of humans) to be achievable. Even for reproduction it requires humans to work together to grow the population.

## Organization & Success

Well organized civilizations are most likely to succeed then the disorganized one, and this may not apply for the entire population and rather a subset of it, which of course stems from the economic policies that are existent in the society that favor different characteristics and attributes for a person to succeed in the given civilization.

## Economic Sphere

This is essentially the bedrock of civilizations. Every civilization requires economic ground for its survival. The primary drive and need of human beings are to survive.

In brief, the economy comprises of goods and services, we need goods to satisfy our needs and services to meet the other derived wants or needs we have created for ourselves. The existence of these goods and services is triggered by value generated by humans over them.

So essentially when we purchase a product or pay for a service, its because we value it and we want to obtain satisfaction from using it or having possession of it. To produce these goods, it requires proper coordination of: capital, energy, labor and markets.

Proper economic systems stabilize societies, because it meets their demands and wants. Satisfaction of the basic needs of humans, fosters the creation of luxuries which signify a state of met wants and needs and good welfare.

So for instance with social and self-esteem needs have driven for the growth of social media platforms that we see today.

## Political Sphere

Civilizations require structure to prevent chaos. If everyone was free to do what they wanted, then society would break, because without rules there exists no order. I would be unfair or injustice for someone else to be able to harm you without a repercussion for their own actions.

The rise of governance, laws, power distribution, and control mechanisms have been influenced by the instincts of human beings ourselves, and they've been influenced by our own cultures and normalized ways of living.

There are many systems such as monarchies, republics or empires, that have worked to organize the resources available to create a good welfare for the general public. Policies that drive everyday life including education heavily rely on having good political structures that prevent the abuse of power and misuse of resources for the greedy minority.

It is almost hard to leave the greed of power. Many leaders and political figures have fallen prey of this, forcing to lead their nations for unprecedented number of times, however some countries have been saved by their termed conditions and lowered concentration of power to one body.

Surveillance of the society has also grown across the years with the growth of technologies that support other governments to keep an eye on their societies to prevent possible government overthrows which could take them out of power and destabilize their authority.

## How Civilizations Evolve

Civilizations evolve as time proceeds. It may grow significantly and evolve to something else, or may come to a collapse. Both actions do not take place at a sudden. And occur as a cumulation of stress and becomes amplified by the effect or political powers.

## Causes of Collapse

The collapse of civilizations can be caused by economic factors such as resource depletion or trade disruptions that lead to famine and unrest.

In terms of social aspects, inequality and social conflicts or cultural shifts may trigger decline of civilizations.

Politically, overextension of power, corruption and fragmented control over society can shake powerful systems out of place creating disorder in the society.

External factors such as invasions, and climate change or technological lags can significantly bring down a civilization. New technological breakthroughs can also trigger new changes to society which may drive for new ways of existence.
